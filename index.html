About the Game

Heritage Quest: A 2.5D Gamified Digital Archive for Cultural Preservation in Region 12 is a capstone project that turns local history into play. Players travel across South Cotabato, Sarangani, Sultan Kudarat, and Cotabato, meeting characters and collecting cultural knowledge along the way.

Every discovery is saved to an in-game archive, so learning about the traditions, places, and stories of Region 12 becomes part of the adventure instead of a separate lesson.

Features

2.5D Exploration

Walk through isometric-style scenes inspired by real places in Region 12.

Discovery Journal

Every cultural item you find is recorded in your digital archive.

Quests & Minigames

Finish quests and challenges, including the Mt. Apo climb.

Stats & Survival

Manage your stamina and resources as you travel.

Cultural Preservation

Learn authentic stories, tools, and traditions from the region.

How to install

  1. Download the ZIP file.
  2. Right-click it and choose Extract All.
  3. Open the extracted game folder.
  4. Run the game executable (HeritageQuest.exe).

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 4 GB RAM or more
  • Any graphics card with OpenGL 3.3 support
  • Internet connection (for online features)
